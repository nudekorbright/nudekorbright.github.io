About Me

The analyst who speaks
business fluently.

I am Bright Nudekor, a Marketing BI Data Analyst based in Cairo, Egypt — on a mission to help businesses stop guessing and start making decisions backed by real data.

My journey started with Excel, expanded into SQL and Power BI, and is backed by a deep commitment to understanding not just the tools but the business questions behind every dataset.

I do not just deliver charts and tables. Every analysis I produce comes with clear insights and specific recommendations — because data only has value when it drives action.

My specialty is marketing analytics — helping ecommerce businesses and marketing teams understand which campaigns are making money, who their best customers are, and where their next growth opportunity is hiding.
